What it does

One app for the people and stock side of the business.

Built for teams that cannot rely on a steady connection. Everything keeps working, and catches up on its own.

HR and employees

Employee records, contracts and departments, with access that follows each person's role.

Payroll

Pay runs and payslips, with rates and rules applied when the record is written, so past pay stays correct.

Warehouse and stock

Goods receipts, transfers between locations, stocktakes and imports from a spreadsheet.

Works offline

Keep working with no internet. Changes wait on your computer and sync as soon as you reconnect.

Locked down

The local database is encrypted. Sensitive actions ask for your password again before they go through.

Updates itself

New versions arrive automatically and are checked against a signature before they install. It never updates while changes are still unsynced.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Matrix Hub is used by teams, and your administrator creates your account and decides what you can see and change.

Do I need internet?

Not to keep working. You need it to sign in, to sync your changes with the rest of the team, and to receive updates.

How do updates work?

The app checks for a new version when you sign in and shows a banner. One click installs it and restarts the app. The update is verified against a signature first, and the app will not update while you have changes that have not synced yet.

Is my data safe on my computer?

The local database is encrypted, and its key is kept in the Windows credential vault, not in a file.

Windows warned me about the installer. Is that normal?

Yes, for a new app that Windows has not seen many times. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Where do I get help?

Ask your administrator first. For anything else, write to info@mtrxco.com.
